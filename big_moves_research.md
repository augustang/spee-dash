Iran's new Supreme Leader declared the Strait of Hormuz — carrying ~20% of global oil supply — effectively closed, driving oil from ~$70 pre-conflict to over $113 at the peak. ## Pattern Notes

### The two dominant catalysts
1. **Tariff policy (2025)** — 21 of 33 big moves were either a tariff shock or tariff relief bounce. Every major escalation (Liberation Day, China 104%, China 125%) produced multi-day clusters of ±1.5% days. Relief announcements (90-day pause, EU delay, US-China deal) produced some of the biggest single-day rallies in years.

2. **Geopolitical / oil (2026)** — The Iran conflict compressed all of 2026's volatility into a tight March–April window. Moves came fast (−1.7%, −1.7%, then +2.9%, +2.5% over two weeks). The speed matters for 0DTE positioning.

### What this means for 0DTE put spreads
- Most sharp down days (−2% or more) were **headline-driven**, not slow fundamental deterioration — they happened within a single session, often in the first hour after a news release.
- **Down moves cluster.** Apr 3, 4, 8 were all ±1.5%+ in the same week. Once a macro shock starts, it tends to continue for days. That's the risk SpotGamma's Risk Pivot level is designed to flag.
- **Up moves are more front-loaded.** The first day of a relief rally is usually the biggest (Apr 9, May 12, Mar 31).
